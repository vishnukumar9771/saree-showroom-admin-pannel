## Brand & Style
This design system marries the imperial gravitas of royal Indian textile heritage with the rigorous precision of high-performance SaaS instrumentation. Tailored specifically for managing bespoke weaves (Kanjeevaram, Banarasi, Tussar, and Chanderi), the interface speaks to curatorial authority, master craftsmanship, and operational clarity.

The visual language draws from **Modern Luxury Minimalism** accented with **Editorial Tactility**:
- **Atmosphere:** Deep, contemplative, and opulent without visual noise. Evokes private salons and archive registries.
- **Form:** Razor-sharp architectural discipline contrasted with subtle hairline dividers reminiscent of fine zari threads.
- **Emotion:** Dignified, immaculate, discerning, and effortless. It balances sensory richness with zero operational friction.

## Colors
The color architecture rests upon museum-grade warmth rather than clinical starkness.

- **Primary (`#641E32` - Deep Burgundy):** Reserved for primary interactive accents, focused state indicators, high-level headers, and luxury validation points.
- **Secondary (`#C3A46C` - Antique Gold):** Embodies the radiance of zari threads; applied sparingly to premium flags, high-tier collector indicators, key performance inflection marks, and subtle decorative borders.
- **Tertiary (`#3B1422` - Dark Wine):** Utilized for deep navigation sidebars, high-contrast anchor panels, modal backdrops, and active row indicators.
- **Neutral Core (`#24211F` - Dark Charcoal):** Used for primary data display, typography, and metrics to ensure complete WCAG AAA legibility against light grounds.
- **Surfaces & Canvases:**
  - Base canvas: `#F5F0E8` (Warm Ivory) creating an unbleached raw silk background.
  - Card & Container surface: `#FFFFFF` with ultra-fine `#D8C9B4` (Soft Sand) containment lines.
  - Subtle fills & zebra stripes: `#FAF7F2`.
- **Status & Operational Tokens:**
  - *Paid / Verified:* Hunter Emerald (`#1C4A3A` on `#EBF3EF`) with fine `#95C1AE` borders.
  - *Shipped / Dispatched:* Royal Slate (`#203E5F` on `#ECF2F9`) with fine `#A4C1E4` borders.
  - *Processing / Weaving:* Spiced Ochre (`#7A5212` on `#FAF3E7`) with fine `#E5CFA0` borders.
  - *Pending / Hold:* Muted Sienna (`#6E3725` on `#F8EEEA`) with fine `#DBB8AE` borders.
  - *Low Stock / Atelier Alert:* Crimson Rose (`#8B1E2D` on `#FBECEE`) with fine `#E8A2AA` borders.

## Typography
The system enforces a dual-type hierarchy:

1. **Editorial & Curatorial Voice (Playfair Display):** Applied to high-impact touchpoints: dashboard section titles, collection catalog names, customer names in detail vistas, and brand-facing statement panels. It provides heritage warmth without sacrificing legibility.
2. **Operational Rigor (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Governs data tables, metric tallies, form inputs, navigation links, and administrative controls. High x-height and open counters guarantee effortless scannability even in multi-row inventory tables.
3. **Tabular Numerics:** All price points (e.g., INR ₹2,85,000), SKU counts, warp-weft densities, and analytics utilize standard tabular figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) to maintain vertical alignment in data grids.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows a fluid-responsive architecture anchored by a fixed left navigation structure on desktop surfaces.

- **Grid Framework:**
  - **Desktop (≥ 1280px):** 12-column grid system with `margin: 2rem` and `gutter: 1.5rem`. Left master navigation bar is fixed at 260px width.
  - **Tablet (768px – 1279px):** 8-column layout with `margin: 1.5rem` and `gutter: 1rem`. Sidebar collapses to an icon rail of 72px width.
  - **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with `margin: 1rem` and `gutter: 0.75rem`. Bottom dock or drawer navigation replaces the sidebar.
- **Rhythm & Padding:**
  - Dashboard analytics cards maintain internal padding of `space-lg` (1.5rem) to ensure numbers breathe against their framing borders.
  - Data table cells use vertical padding of `space-sm` (0.5rem) and horizontal padding of `space-md` (1rem) for balanced information density.

## Elevation & Depth
This design avoids thick plastic drop-shadows, using instead an **incised, ambient, and multi-layered depth** approach:

- **Base Layer:** The canvas rests on `#F5F0E8` (Warm Ivory).
- **Surface Layer (Cards, Tables, Panels):** Pure `#FFFFFF` surfaces lifted subtly from the base using an ultra-fine border (`1px solid #D8C9B4`) paired with an ambient tinted shadow:
  - `0 1px 3px rgba(36, 33, 31, 0.04), 0 6px 16px -4px rgba(100, 30, 50, 0.03)`
- **Hover & Active States:** Rather than expanding shadows, cards highlight with a razor-thin border shift from `#D8C9B4` to `#C3A46C` (Antique Gold) accompanied by an ambient shift:
  - `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(59, 20, 34, 0.08)`
- **Modals & Drawers:** Deep overlay scrim utilizing `#3B1422` with 40% opacity and a backdrop blur of `4px`. Drawer bodies emerge with an outline of `1px solid rgba(195, 164, 108, 0.35)`.

## Shapes
The shape language draws inspiration from tailored luxury packaging, bespoke saree boxes, and letterpress certificates. 

- **Structure:** Level `1` (Soft - 0.25rem / 4px base radius). Surfaces remain crisp, disciplined, and refined. Circular elements are strictly prohibited for cards or modal containers.
- **Exceptions:**
  - Pill shapes (`rounded-full`) are permitted solely for operational status tags, metric percentage badges, and collector profile avatars.
  - Input fields, data tables, and major structural boundaries retain the 4px soft radius to maintain architectural dignity.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background in `#641E32` (Deep Burgundy), text in `#FFFFFF`, subtle 1px border in `#3B1422`. Hover transitions to `#3B1422` with an inner border glow in `#C3A46C`.
- **Secondary / Heritage Action:** Background transparent, text `#641E32`, border `1px solid #C3A46C`. Hover fills with `#FAF3E7`.
- **Ghost / Neutral:** Background transparent, text `#24211F`, border `1px solid transparent`. Hover reveals `1px solid #D8C9B4` and background `#F5F0E8`.

### Status Badges & Chips
- Formed with `border-radius: 9999px` (Pill-shaped), uppercase tracking via `label-sm`, and internal padding of `0.25rem 0.65rem`.
- Always rendered with high-contrast text on tinted, desaturated backgrounds accompanied by a 1px border matching the tint:
  - **Paid:** `#1C4A3A` on `#EBF3EF` (Border: `#95C1AE`)
  - **Shipped:** `#203E5F` on `#ECF2F9` (Border: `#A4C1E4`)
  - **Processing:** `#7A5212` on `#FAF3E7` (Border: `#E5CFA0`)
  - **Pending:** `#6E3725` on `#F8EEEA` (Border: `#DBB8AE`)
  - **Low Stock:** `#8B1E2D` on `#FBECEE` (Border: `#E8A2AA`)

### Input Fields & Controls
- Base background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #D8C9B4`, text `#24211F`, placeholder `#8D857B`.
- Focused state applies `border-color: #641E32` and an outer ring of `2px solid rgba(195, 164, 108, 0.25)`.
- Checkboxes and radios use a fine square border (`#C3A46C`) with active fill in `#641E32`.

### Data Tables & Lists
- Table headers utilize `#FAF7F2` backgrounds with uppercase typography (`label-md`) in `#641E32`, delimited by a bottom border of `1px solid #D8C9B4`.
- Alternating row zebra striping (optional): `#FFFFFF` and `#FCFAF7`.
- Row hover triggers a subtle gold wash: `background-color: rgba(195, 164, 108, 0.06)`.
- Columns featuring saree imagery use 40x52px thumbnails (matching the 3:4 portrait ratio of folded drapes) framed in a 1px `#D8C9B4` border.

### Metric & Analytics Cards
- Flat `#FFFFFF` surface framed by `1px solid #D8C9B4`.
- Top-level label in `label-md` tracking wide, primary figure rendered in `metric-display` (`#24211F`), accompanied by small vector trend arrows tinted in `#1C4A3A` (gain) or `#8B1E2D` (loss).
- An optional top accent border (`2px solid #C3A46C`) highlights core revenue and archive metrics.

### Saree Provenance Card (Specialty Admin Component)
- A bespoke layout card showcasing master weaver identification, loom location, zari purity certificate badges (e.g., *Tested Zari / Pure Gold 0.6%*), and real-time inventory allocation.
